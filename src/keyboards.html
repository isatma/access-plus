Shared keyboard library

Virtual keyboards for OptiKey, made to share

OptiKey is a free, open-source on-screen keyboard and mouse that works with eye gaze, head or nose tracking, or a mouse. Its custom keyboards are small files anyone can make, so a layout that works for one person can help many others.

Browse layouts built by users and therapists, try them in the computer simulator, download the ones you like, adapt them, and share your version back. Your keyboard settings, like dwell time and key size, travel with your Access+ profile.

About OptiKey: free and open source (GPL 3), for Windows, works with eye trackers, a webcam or a mouse. Created by Julius Sweetland, inspired by his aunt's motor neuron disease (ALS).

Share your keyboard

Made a layout that works well? Share it so others with similar needs can start from something proven instead of from scratch.

  • What to include: the keyboard file, a picture, and a sentence on who it helps.
  • Inputs it suits: eye gaze, nose or head tracking, mouse, or switch scanning.
  • Positions: seated, tilted, or in bed, since key size and placement often change.
  • Credit and license: your name or a nickname, and permission for others to adapt it.

OptiKey is an independent open-source project. Access+ doesn't make or sell it; the library shares layouts made for it.
