Music, art and movement, hands-free

Works in progress, shared as demos while they are rebuilt. Experimental apps prepared for ISATMA, the International Symposium on Assistive Technology, Music and Art. Each one uses colorful on-screen targets you can trigger with eye tracking, a head pointer, a switch, touch, or movement picked up by a webcam. Some respond to whole-body motion.

As-is prototype software

Provided as is, without warranty of any kind, for demonstration and discussion. Each app carries its own license notice: source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Camera processing happens in your browser; the apps state that nothing is recorded or uploaded. Camera features may not work inside this private preview, which can block webcam access; touch, mouse, switch and dwell still work.

In development · description only for now

Song Boards Suite

The next generation of Songboards, being rebuilt as a suite of three apps that share one hands-free engine. It runs from a small local web server on your own computer, which keeps the camera and the Save As folder picker working, and a one-time setup downloads its open-source libraries so it can run fully offline.

For developersThese apps are published unchanged except for a few hosting fixes: Songboards opens on a music board and reports if its sound library cannot load, the hand-tracking ergometer loads fixed versions of its hand-tracking library, the Musical Dial (v9.1) adds built-in sound, dwell on the key ring and screen fitting, Mouse Orchestra (v6.1) adds a start-sound button, tap support and a hover delay, and the Mini Color Keyboard adds built-in sound alongside MIDI, the Virtual Harmonica (v7.1) adds a start button, the built-in synth by default, a mouse or touch mode and screen fitting.
