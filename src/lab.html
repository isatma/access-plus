Music, art and movement, hands-free

Experimental apps prepared for ISATMA, the International Symposium on Assistive Technology, Music and Art. Each one uses colorful on-screen targets you can trigger with eye tracking, a head pointer, a switch, touch, or movement picked up by a webcam. Some respond to whole-body motion.

As-is prototype software

Provided as is, without warranty of any kind, for demonstration and discussion. Each app carries its own license notice: source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Camera processing happens in your browser; the apps state that nothing is recorded or uploaded. Camera features may not work inside this private preview, which can block webcam access; touch, mouse, switch and dwell still work.

For developersThese apps are published unchanged except for two hosting fixes: the hand-tracking ergometer loads fixed versions of its hand-tracking library, the Musical Dial (v9.1) adds built-in sound, dwell on the key ring and screen fitting, and Mouse Orchestra (v6.1) adds a start-sound button, tap support and a hover delay. Build scripts and work-in-progress files are kept out of the lab. Open the original Accessibility Studio home.
