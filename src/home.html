Your AI agent

An AI agent that helps you build skills at every step: finding a starting point, coaching practice, tuning your setup and tracking progress.

  • Gets to know youAsks what you want to do and finds your most reliable way to control technology.
  • Follows you across devicesOne profile for your computer, phone, chair and home, and it notices early when your needs change.
  • You stay in controlEvery suggestion is explained, you decide, and your practice becomes evidence for your therapist and funders.
ACCESS+ AI AGENTExample
I'm in bed this week and nose tracking keeps drifting.
Lying down changes your range of motion. Let's make an in-bed profile: I'll re-center your rest position and widen the dead zone a little. Then try two minutes of the pointing exercise and I'll compare it with your seated scores.

The guide uses Claude on your own Claude account. It's for learning and practice, not medical advice.
