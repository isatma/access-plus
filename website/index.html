Meet Ace, your AI guide

Ace, the Adaptive Control Expert, is an AI-powered coach that helps you build skills at every step.

  • Finds a starting pointAsks about your goals, movement and positions, then suggests inputs and simulators to try.
  • Coaches while you practiceGives tips during simulator sessions and adjusts the difficulty as you improve.
  • Tunes your setupRecommends calibration changes such as dead zone, dwell time and sensitivity, based on how you did.
  • Helps you test and connectWalks you through connecting a sensor from your kit and checking it works.
  • Tracks your progressSummarizes results for you, your family or your therapist. You control what it saves.
