🎵 Songboards

Hands-free music for everyone. Songboards turns assistive technology into musical instruments. Build a virtual keyboard — colorful targets that play notes, chords, and drums — then play it with a head tracker, an eye tracker, an assistive switch, a stylus, a mouse, or just a finger. Learn a song on your own, then play it together with other people — a duet, trio, quartet, band, or whole orchestra, each person on their own device. A growing toolkit for making music accessible to people with disabilities.

Ways to play

  • 🧭 Head pointer or head tracker
  • 👁️ Eye tracker / eye gaze
  • 🔘 Assistive switch
  • ✏️ Stylus
  • 🖱️ Mouse
  • 👆 Finger or touch

🎯 Target Editor

Examples: C4 · C4, E4, G4 · D#5 · Bb3
1
Each hover appends this many bars of the pattern to the drum chain at the bottom of the stage. The chain plays bar by bar in perfect time — keep hovering to keep it going, or finish with a Cymbal Crash. Latency-free.
Type chord names separated by spaces. Supports major (C), minor (Dm, Am, Em), sharps/flats (F#, Bb), and 7ths (the 7 is ignored, triad is played). Leave blank to use the dropdown above.
Hover over a Start target to begin the progression. Hover over a Stop target — or the red Mute target, or the Off button — to silence it.
Reward targets also play any notes you set above — leave Notes empty for a silent celebration.

Activate this target by camera wave Turn on the 📷 Cam toolbar button, then wave your hand over this target in the camera view to fire it.
⏱ Dwell instead of wave (more forgiving) Fires when your hand stays inside the target for the dwell time. Better for limited movement or tremor. No waving needed.

👥 Group Play

Play one song together — as a duet, trio, quartet, band, or whole orchestra. Each person opens this songboard on their own phone, picks a part, and you play together in the same room. No internet, no accounts.

Which part are you playing?

Tap your part. Tell the others which one you picked so everyone has a different part. You can change it any time.
