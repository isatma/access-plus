💃 Dance Board

Move and paint with your motion. Your webcam watches how much you're moving — arms, head, shoulders, whatever you've got — and turns it into colorful visual effects on screen. The more you move, the more vivid the canvas becomes.

  • 🎨 Paint trails — motion leaves brush strokes of color that fade gradually
  • 🌸 Bloom rings — each big movement opens a glowing ring outward
  • 🌊 Color waves — gentle horizontal sweeps that track your activity
  • ✨ Constellation — your motions place stars that connect with thin lines
  • 🎉 Confetti burst — every motion peak shoots colored particles
  • 🌌 Aurora wash — slow, ambient color drifts that respond to motion
  • 🎗️ Ribbons — fluid streamers that follow the rhythm of your moves
  • 🎆 Fireworks — celebratory bursts on big motion peaks

A real-time energy meter tracks how much you've moved. A dance score rewards both peak intensity and sustained activity — a measurable, expressive way to capture movement.

Your camera feed is processed locally in your browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Stop at any time.
