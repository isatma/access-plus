## Version 0.1 · Pilot draft · October 31, 2026

First pilot website for feedback.

- Home page: headline, guide arm (navigate the site or pick up a cup, phone or remote), simulator cards, how it works, goals, open to everyone, site navigation.
- Meet Ace: live AI guide chat (Ace, Adaptive Control Expert) with an animated wireframe face.

- My profile: sample profile and a profile built by talking with Ace (skills, current tools with Ace's suggestions, movement, positions, goals, support, next steps).
- Gesture check: camera self-assessment for smile, open mouth, eyebrows, long blink, pucker, head tilt, turn and nod, plus a nose pointer test. For embedding on the Access+ Google page.
